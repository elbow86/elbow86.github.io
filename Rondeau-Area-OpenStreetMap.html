How To Use It

  • Use the shaded areas as trip clusters rather than turn-by-turn routes.
  • Use the blue dashed route as the ferry leg to Pelee Island.
  • Keep Point Pelee and Pelee Island as separate outings.
  • Click any marker for notes and an OpenStreetMap link.

Coordinate Notes

Mainland markers and the resolved island business markers came from OpenStreetMap Nominatim. West Dock, Lighthouse Point, and Fish Point are approximate manual placements on the OSM base map because those names did not resolve cleanly in Nominatim. Shaded clusters are planning areas, not route geometry.
